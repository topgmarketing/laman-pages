Privacy Policy

Laman Companion · Last updated 7 October 2026

Summary

Laman Companion is a single-user personal analytics tool. It does not serve the public, does not track visitors, and does not sell or share data.

What is collected

  • Analytics statistics — views, likes, comments, shares and similar metrics for content posted on accounts the owner controls.
  • Authentication tokens — stored locally on the owner's own computer to keep API access working.

What is NOT collected

  • No data about visitors to these pages.
  • No cookies, no analytics scripts, no tracking pixels.
  • No personal data about viewers, followers or third parties.
  • No data is sold, shared or transmitted to third parties.

Where data is stored

All data is stored on the owner's local computer. Access tokens are stored in a local configuration file that is not published.

Third-party platforms

Data is retrieved via the official APIs of YouTube, TikTok and Instagram, each governed by their respective privacy policies and developer terms.

Retention

Cached statistics are kept for trend analysis. Data may be deleted at any time by removing the local cache folder.
